WindSpot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

WindSpot is an iOS weather and planning app for skydivers. It has no accounts and no advertising. This policy describes what the app does with location and related data. It matches the disclosures in WindSpot Settings and the app’s privacy manifest.

Data WindSpot uses

  • Precise location (optional). If you choose Use My Location, WindSpot reads your device location while the app is in use to compute straight-line distance to drop zones. Search and custom places do not require location permission.
  • Selected coordinates. When you load weather for a place (saved place, search result, or device location), WindSpot sends that latitude and longitude to weather services so they can return forecasts and observations for that point or nearby area.
  • On-device preferences and places. Units, model choices, similar settings, named places, and cached weather snapshots stay on your device (UserDefaults / local storage). They are not uploaded to a WindSpot server. WindSpot does not operate a user account backend.

Who receives coordinates

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — model winds aloft, Outlook meteogram data, and optional terrain elevation estimates.
  • NOAA / NWS Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov) — airport observations (METAR), forecasts (TAF), and airport records.
  • Apple — MapKit map tiles and map services when maps are shown; a custom place may also use Apple services for time zone.

Requests use HTTPS. WindSpot does not sell your data and does not use your location to track you across apps or websites. Location is not linked to an account or other identity in WindSpot, because WindSpot has no accounts.

Purpose

Location and coordinates are used only for app functionality: weather for the place you selected, map display, drop-zone distance, and related planning features (including instructional Spot estimates from model winds and your confirmed landing / jump-run inputs).

What WindSpot does not do

  • No user accounts, login, or Sign in with Apple.
  • No advertising network, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting service is built into the app as described in current Settings disclosures.
  • No tracking as defined by Apple’s App Privacy / privacy manifest (NSPrivacyTracking is false).

Third-party terms

Open-Meteo and NOAA process requests under their own terms. Open-Meteo data is credited under CC BY 4.0 in the app. Map data and services are provided by Apple. WindSpot is not affiliated with Open-Meteo, NOAA, USPA, Windy, or markschulze.net.

Children

WindSpot is a weather and skydiving planning tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children for accounts or profiles; it has no accounts.

Changes

If WindSpot’s data practices change (for example a commercial Open-Meteo plan, analytics, or accounts), this page will be updated and in-app disclosures should be updated to match before relying on new practices.
