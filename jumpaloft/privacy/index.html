JumpAloft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

JumpAloft is an iOS weather and planning app for skydivers. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. This policy describes what the app sends, to whom, and why. It matches the disclosure in JumpAloft Settings and the app’s privacy manifest.

What leaves your iPhone

  • The drop zone you select. Its directory id is sent to the JumpAloft API (api.jumpaloft.com) to load winds aloft, the Outlook and the wind map for it.
  • Coordinates of a place you add. For a custom place, its coordinates are sent to the JumpAloft API, which rounds them to a cell of about 2 km and samples forecasts there. The cell is shared by everyone who saves a place in it and is not linked to you. It stops being sampled after 180 days without use. The coordinates may also be used to look up the ground elevation (JumpAloft API) and the time zone (Apple).
  • Nearby airport reports. The selected place’s coordinates are sent to the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov) to find nearby airport observations (METAR) and pilot reports.
  • The map area you view. The wind map loads its base map (OpenFreeMap), terrain shading (public elevation tiles) and radar and satellite images through the JumpAloft API, so those services do not see your device. Other maps in the app are Apple’s (MapKit).

Your device location

JumpAloft asks for location only while in use, and uses it only when you ask:

  • Use My Location measures straight-line distance to drop zones, on your iPhone.
  • Cutaway Finder: during a search you start, your location points you to your canopy and records where you have searched, also with the phone locked, until you pause or end the search or tap Found It. The search track and your finds stay on your iPhone. They leave it only if you share the search map yourself. Finds you mark where you stand are kept on your iPhone to improve future estimates.

Your device location is not sent to the JumpAloft API or any other service.

What the JumpAloft API records

The JumpAloft API runs on Cloudflare. For a sample of requests it records usage statistics: the kind of request, the drop zone id, the forecast model, how long it took and whether it succeeded, and an approximate location derived by Cloudflare from the network address (country, region, city, and a position rounded to about 50 km). Your IP address is not stored, and nothing is linked to you or your device. These statistics are used to keep the service running and to see which drop zones and features are used.

On your iPhone only

Saved places, favorites, settings, Spot setups, reserve repack dates, cached weather, Cutaway Finder searches and finds stay on your device. JumpAloft has no account backend.

What JumpAloft does not do

  • No accounts, login, or Sign in with Apple.
  • No advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.
  • No tracking as defined by Apple (NSPrivacyTracking is false), and no selling or sharing of data.

Data sources and credits

Forecasts come from NOAA (RRFS, HRRR, GFS, MRMS radar, GOES satellite) and ECMWF open data (CC BY 4.0), served by the JumpAloft API. Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), OpenMapTiles, OpenFreeMap. Each provider processes requests under its own terms.

Children

JumpAloft is a weather and skydiving planning tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children; it has no accounts.

Changes

If JumpAloft’s data practices change, this page and the in-app disclosure are updated before the change ships.
